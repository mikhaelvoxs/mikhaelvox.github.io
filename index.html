Biography

The story behind the voice.

Chukwu Obinna Michael, known professionally as Mikhaelvox, is a Nigerian gospel and worship artist whose journey is rooted in a lifelong love for music, worship and the presence of God.

Born on 19 November 2006 in Epe, Lagos State, Chukwu Obinna Michael was born into the family of Mr. and Mrs. Israel Chukwu as the fifth child. From an early age, music was not simply something he listened to; it was something he felt. He was drawn to the atmosphere created by sound, worship and melody, and he began to discover that music could move the heart in a way ordinary words sometimes could not.

His love for music started very early. At about six years old, he already carried a strong passion for singing. As he grew, that passion developed into a deeper love for worship. The atmosphere of music could move him deeply, and worship became a place where he could be intentional, quiet his heart and feel spiritually connected.

His educational journey began at St. Michael Anglican Primary School, Epe. He later attended Army Children Junior Secondary School, Epe, before continuing to Government Technical College, Odomola, Epe.

Life took a painful turn in 2022 when he lost his father. The loss brought a difficult season and made it hard to continue life in the same way. His education was interrupted while he was at Tech 2. In the middle of that uncertainty, he travelled to Abuja to stay with his brother, and eventually continued his secondary education at Government Secondary School, Gwantu, Kaduna State.

That chapter became part of the story behind the artist he is becoming. The journey has not been without battles, pain or seasons of uncertainty. Yet, through every stage, his faith and love for worship have remained a source of direction. His testimony is not that everything has already become perfect; it is that grace has kept him moving.

Mikhaelvox is still growing, still learning and still building. He openly carries the conviction that he is not yet where he wants to be, but he is also no longer where he used to be. For him, that progress is by the grace of God.

“I am not where I want to be, but I am not where I used to be. It is all by the grace of God.”
My Journey

From passion to purpose.

A childhood passion

At about six years old, Mikhaelvox began singing and carrying a natural passion for music. What started as an early love gradually became something deeper: a desire to use his voice intentionally.

Through the pain

The loss of his father in 2022 changed the direction of his life. Education was interrupted, and a new chapter began away from home. Through the difficult season, the dream did not die.

The name Mikhaelvox

The name Mikhaelvox is deeply personal. It grew from the name Michael and represents the artistic voice emerging from that identity. It is more than a stage name; it is the name attached to a growing sound and calling.

Why worship?

Worship is where Mikhaelvox feels most intentional and at peace. He is drawn to the atmosphere of worship because it allows music to become prayer, surrender and spiritual expression.

The Sound

Prophetic. African. Soaking. Revival.

Mikhaelvox is building a sound around prophetic worship, African worship, soaking worship, spiritual chants and revival. The goal is not simply to make songs, but to create an atmosphere where people can slow down, worship and turn their hearts toward God.

Prophetic Worship

Worship expressed with spiritual intention and a heart open to God's presence.

African Worship

A sound connected to African rhythm, expression and the depth of communal worship.

Soaking Worship

Music that creates room for stillness, prayer, reflection and surrender.

Revival & Chants

Simple, repeatable spiritual expressions built to stir hunger for God.

The Burning Altar

A place where worship becomes a meeting point.

The Burning Altar is an upcoming label and creative worship platform connected to the vision of Mikhaelvox. It represents a desire to create a place where people can come together through music, worship, prayer and spiritual expression.

The vision is bigger than a name. The Burning Altar is about connection: connecting people in the room, connecting hearts to worship, and creating spaces where the presence of God remains the center.

As the journey grows, The Burning Altar will become part of the wider story — a home for worship, sound, creativity, revival and people who are hungry for God.

The Vision

Heaven Racing.

The dream is to become a gospel artist burning for Christ, using music to draw the souls of men toward God and keeping heaven as the destination. Mikhaelvox wants the journey to point beyond the artist — toward Christ, worship, salvation, purpose and a life lived intentionally for God.

The road is still being built. The music has not all been released. The platform is still growing. But the vision is already clear: keep burning, keep worshipping, keep growing and keep racing toward heaven.

A Message From Mikhaelvox

If you encounter my music, encounter more than sound.

I want people to find a place where they can breathe, worship and remember God. I want my music to speak to people in their real seasons — in joy, in pain, in waiting, in weakness and in hope. I want the sound to carry worship, not just performance; prayer, not just words; and a hunger for God, not just entertainment.

My prayer is that every song, chant, worship moment and future project will point people back to Christ. If someone listens and becomes hungry for God, finds strength to continue, remembers that grace is still working, or chooses to surrender again, then the music has served its purpose.
